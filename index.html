01 STARTUP Startup Short press → Long press → Wait for standing Startup sequence

Position the body and legs as specified and check that the joints are in normal condition. After pressing the power button, allow initialization to finish; this robot stands up automatically.

⏻

Starting this robot

Based on lab operating notes and the Unitree Go2 user manual.

Sources checked
  1. 01

    Position the body and legs

    Place the robot on level ground with its abdominal support pad touching the ground and its body horizontal. Fully fold the lower legs and rest all leg joints and feet flat. Keep the body from resting on the thighs or shins, and check that all joints are in normal condition.

  2. 02

    Short press, then hold the power button

    Press once briefly, then hold for at least 2 seconds. On this unit, the joints and fans become audible as startup begins. Continue waiting after you hear them.

  3. 03

    Wait for automatic standing

    This robot stands up automatically after startup. Unitree gives an approximate wait of 2 minutes. During normal startup, the head indicator changes from flashing green to steady green and the body remains level. Use the actual robot state as the reference.

Startup posture · Source

The Unitree Go2 user manual specifies the startup posture and says to check it first if startup fails. The positioning instructions above follow that manual.

02 CONTROLLER Controller Official handheld controller · R1 / R3 Official controller

Use the official Unitree two-hand controller. These instructions follow the current official manual for R1 / R3. Functions depend on the installed firmware and the instructions in the app.

Power and connection Official instructions
  1. 01

    Turn on the controller

    Press the power button briefly, then hold for more than 2 seconds. In sound mode, R3 beeps twice at startup; R1 beeps once.

  2. 02

    Confirm the data link

    A paired controller can connect to the robot after powering on. On R3, check the right-side DL indicator; on R1, all left-side data-link signal indicators should be lit.

  3. 03

    Turn off the controller

    Press the power button briefly, then hold for more than 2 seconds. In sound mode, it beeps three times.

Calibration, charging and versions

Joystick calibration

Keep both sticks still, then press and release F1 and F3 together. When you hear one beep per second, move each stick to its full travel and rotate it several times. After the beeping stops, tap F3 to finish calibration.

Charging

Turn the controller off and connect a 5V USB charger to its USB-C port. When fully charged, all battery indicators are on for R3; the older R1 manual says they turn off. Follow the instructions for your version.

Version scope

The current Unitree manual covers R1 and R3 and describes the same core operations. Additional actions can vary with firmware. See the official manual or Unitree Go app for the complete action table.

With ROS 2: publish a stop request

The official endpoint is /api/sport/request, with message type unitree_api/msg/Request. A generic /cmd_vel is converted to official requests only if a separate conversion node is running.

ROS 2: send one stop-motion request
